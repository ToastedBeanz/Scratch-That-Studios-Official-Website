ABOUT

Scratch That Studios

Scratch That studios is a "mega-collaboration" first designed and founded by Josh. (@joshisaurio) on January 7th.

Origin of Scratch That Studios

It all began when Josh, the founder, had a vision, a bright vision. He envisioned that a giant collaboration, while arduous, would work. After a mind-crunching amount of failed attempts, on the dawn of January 7, 2026, he created STS as a way to express creativity and collaboration via games and the development that goes in them. And when it was first founded on the 7th, it was still small and juvenile, and lacked members and creativity.

This is when Fusty, a soon-to-be manager joined and made the first ever application to join STS. He applied to be an artist and was promptly accepted.

After some time on the same day, HeroRyan, who was a manager and scripter, also applied and was accepted.

And slowly but surely, over the course of 2 weeks, Scratch That garnered more and more attention and more people began to join and make their applications. The requirements at the time were very relaxed, as Scratch That was still very new and not yet able to enforce any type of standards due to the lack of people and manpower. However, on the 12th of January, everything was split.

The Split.

On the fated 12th, Josh decided that we had collected enough people, and split the teams into two. The engine team, and the game team. I, Priospire, on that day, was split into the engine team. Fusty, who will be relevant later, was split into the game team. HeroRyan was automatically made the engine team lead by Josh, and we began progress immediately. Over the course of over a month as of the time of writing, Feb 1, 2026; the first few fragments of the engine were developed and published to the private library of STS.

In contrast, the game team was still in its very infant stages, having not yet developed the framework, Priospire rallied up the chat and for just a few minutes, the game team chat was active. Josh took heavy advantage of this, delegating important work to each member, having them publish progress, and sourcing each completed project into a forum. At this time, Todd, an artist, wanted to keep the game team as active as the engine team, if not, even more so. So he applied to be a manager, and was accepted temporarily, to be replaced by Fusty, a master programmer and now-manager of the game team today.

Where We Are Today

Scratch That Studios is, as of February 1st 2026, a semi-known megacollaboration within the "Scratch Coders" community. We have ambitious plans to grow, congregate, and unite the Scratch community through games, and the development behind it all, and you can too! Join us in the pursuit of igniting the hearts of every Scratcher with code, games, music, art, and animation.
